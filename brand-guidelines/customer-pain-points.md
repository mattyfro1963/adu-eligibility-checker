# Customer Pain Points

Persona × pain × message × CTA. Use this when writing landing copy, email sequences, connect flows, or lead forms.

| Pain | Emotional hook | doihave.space answer | CTA direction |
|------|----------------|----------------------|---------------|
| "Can I legally add an ADU?" | Overwhelmed by zoning jargon and conflicting blog posts | Statute-grounded Eligible / Warning / Restricted in one address search | Enter address → Evaluate |

## Shared objections and rebuttals

| Objection | Rebuttal (voice: precise, not defensive) |
|-----------|------------------------------------------|
| "It says eligible but Planning disagreed." | The engine shows the statewide ADU floor and published guidance. Local overlays, design review, and project-specific conditions can still apply — confirm with Planning. |
| "Why is SB 9 still warning?" | Lot zoning was not verified for this coordinate. SB 9 requires single-family residential context — warning until GIS confirms the base district. |
| "Are affiliate products endorsed?" | Featured resources are curated research aids. Confirm local rules before purchase. Not official partnerships. |
| "Is this legal advice?" | No. Informational only — not a permit. Use cited reasons to prepare questions for your planning department or attorney. |
| "Loopholes sound shady." | We mean lawful pathways and hard stops under statute — not workarounds that dodge permits. |

## Message themes by outcome

Use when writing outcome-specific CTAs (see [tone-of-voice.md](./tone-of-voice.md) and [copy-prompts.md](./copy-prompts.md)).

| `overall` | User mindset | Message theme | Primary CTA voice |
|-----------|--------------|---------------|-------------------|
| **eligible** | Momentum — ready to plan or buy | "Statute floor supports your path — confirm design details locally" | Outfit the build / browse partners |
| **warning** | Caution — overlays or unverified zoning | "Proceed with eyes open — specialist can interpret diagnostics" | Request specialist review (amber) |
| **restricted** | Blocked or narrow paths | "Engine shows hard stops — expert review for alternate pathways" | Request Review (rose) |

## Emotional hooks to reuse (sparingly)

- "Before you buy, deliver, or pour a foundation…"
- "One search. Cited reasons. No canned eligibility."
- "All CA counties — free county requirements."
- "Confirm with Planning — we help you ask the right questions."

Avoid fear-mongering. Restricted status is factual, not punitive.
